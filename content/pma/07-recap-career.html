Special topics from the recap

Financing can shape world history: St. Peter's Basilica

Möller's favourite excursus on Lecture 2 — "Financing of major projects can influence (world) politics and history!"

  • Construction of the present basilica began in 1506 and was completed in 1626.
  • The financing of St. Peter's Basilica was one of the main triggers of the Reformation: in order to finance the enormous construction costs, the sale of indulgences ("Ablasshandel") was one of the main sources of financing.
  • The publication of Martin Luther's 95 Theses in 1517 is considered the beginning of the Reformation:
    • "Therefore, those preachers of indulgences are mistaken who say that a person is released from all punishment and saved through indulgences from the Pope."
    • "Those who say that the soul rises from purgatory as soon as the coin falls into the box with a clink are preaching lies and deception."

Cultural challenges from practice

Language barrier
  • incorrect execution of instructions due to misunderstanding of technical terms
  • delays because of the need to repeat or clarify messages multiple times
  • difficulty expressing complex ideas or feedback, especially under pressure
  • frustration or tension among colleagues due to repeated misunderstandings
  • hesitation to speak up in meetings out of fear of making mistakes

The construction industry abroad — what is different

  • Regional conflict — the security situation as a framework condition
  • Heat stress management — the thermal working limit per the ADOSH-SF Technical Guideline "Safety in the Heat"
  • Safety & health and worker's welfare as management fields in their own right
  • Team building
  • Digitalization — Building Information Modeling or digital twin, drones and laser scanning

Key differences Middle East vs. Germany: project types and scale · workforce and labor structure · regulations and standards · sustainability · work culture and team dynamics.
